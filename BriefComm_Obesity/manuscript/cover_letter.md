# Cover letter

21 November 2026

Dear Editors,

Please consider my manuscript, **"Three artefacts manufacture a 'weight-loss toxin rebound' alarm, and total testosterone masks a fall in the free fraction"**, for publication in *Obesity* as a Brief Cutting Edge Report.

**What the paper does.** A widely repeated claim holds that rapid weight loss mobilises stored lipophilic pollutants, which then accelerate biological ageing and blunt the hormonal benefit of losing weight. The evidence for that claim is almost always a significant pollutant-by-weight-change interaction. I began by reproducing the alarm in 45,739 US adults — the crude estimate, the significant heterogeneity of the testosterone response (P=0.0049) and the significant modification of liver-fat scores (P=1.2×10⁻⁶) all appeared in the data. I then showed that each of them is manufactured by a different artefact: confounding (adjustment moves the ageing estimate 2.20 years in the opposite direction), unmeasured prescribing (167 of 2,244 men, 7.4%, on androgen-suppressing drugs, carry the entire interaction), and formulas that contain body mass index (removing the BMI term takes the liver interaction from P=1.2×10⁻⁶ to 0.68). Critically, all three artefacts bias towards harm. That asymmetry is the paper's argument: in this literature, the density of alarms is not a measure of the evidence behind them. An audit of the 12 NHANES studies that have modelled sex hormones against persistent pollutants shows that none screened for GnRH agonists or opioid analgesics.

**Why it belongs in *Obesity*.** What survives the analysis is a reading instruction that our readers can use next week. Per 5% weight loss, total testosterone rose 4.0% while SHBG rose 4.8%, so the free fraction fell 2.3% and calculated free testosterone did not change detectably (+1.6%, P=0.16). In the era of pharmacological weight loss, total testosterone will rise in many patients. A rise from 300 to 312 ng/dL with a falling free fraction is not evidence that gonadal function has recovered. The paper also gives analysts three reusable checks — the adjustment shift, the prescription flag, and the BMI-term decomposition — for a literature that will keep producing these interactions.

**Relationship to other work.** This manuscript is a stand-alone methodological adjudication with a claim that is not made elsewhere. A separate, longer report from the same project describes the tissue-level and structural analyses (adipose single-nucleus RNA sequencing, carrier-protein comparison) and is being prepared independently. The single-cell arm is summarised in the Supplementary Material of this submission for completeness, but it carries no exposure measurement and does not bear on the attribution argument here. All data, code, the 256-row ledger linking every reported number to its source file, and the numbered evidence pack are openly available at https://github.com/YuAn-alt608/-Obesity26.11.11, and I am happy to provide anything the editors wish to inspect.

**Compliance.** The manuscript is original, is not under consideration elsewhere, has not been published previously in whole or in part, and has not been posted as a preprint. The author has approved the submission and declares no competing interests; the work received no specific funding. The study is a secondary analysis of publicly available, de-identified data and was exempt from ethics review; reporting follows STROBE, and the completed checklist is provided as a Supplementary file.
